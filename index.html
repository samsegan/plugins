Plug-ins by

For friends and family · alpha

Ten plug-ins for Logic, Live or any DAW that loads Audio Units or VST3, and four Mac apps that go with them. One line installs the lot, and everything keeps itself up to date.

Get everything

  1. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal and press Return.
  2. Paste this line and press Return:
    curl -fsSL https://samsegan.github.io/plugins/install.sh | bash
  3. Quit and reopen your DAW.

Plug-ins: macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel

No password and no account. It installs every plug-in and app below, skips an app your Mac can't run, and sets up the updates. Run it again whenever you like: it only brings in what's new.

Just the plug-ins

curl -fsSL https://samsegan.github.io/plugins/install.sh | bash -s -- --plugins

Or download the zip and install them by hand: unzip it, type bash and a space in Terminal, drag install.sh from the MusicPlugins folder onto the window and press Return. Plug-ins installed that way don't update themselves.

The plug-ins

Audio Unit and VST3 · Apple silicon and Intel · macOS 11 or later

Breakerizer

Instrument

Builds breaks out of one-shots. Drop in a few samples, or a loop to chop, roll the dice, and drag the break back out as one WAV, stems, MIDI or the kit.

Chopperizer

Slicer

Finds every hit in a drum loop and lets you drag the slices out as WAVs, one or all at once. Spread pans them wider pair by pair.

OTTizer

Compressor

Xfer's OTT rebuilt from measurements, sample for sample, plus an amount per band, mid/side, and a Focus that keeps it off the fizzy top end.

Reverserizer

Reverse reverb

Prints a reverse reverb of what just played, or of a clip you drop on it, as a wet WAV to drag into your song. Uses your Valhalla reverbs too.

Reversedelayerizer

Reverse delay

The same for delays: reverse echoes that build up into the hit, printed to a WAV.

Tremorizer

Tremolo

Tremolo and auto-pan from shapes you draw, free or in time with the song, or a ramp that speeds up and deepens over the bars you set.

Erodizer

Distortion

Ableton Live's Erosion, for Logic and any other DAW, rebuilt from measurements of the real device.

Rackerizer

Rack

A chain of your Audio Unit effects in one slot: type to add one, drag to reorder, and save racks to load again.

REF

Meter

Last on your master bus: loudness, peaks, spectrum and stereo as you play. Drop a reference song on it and it measures each pass against it.

Combiner Capture

For Combiner

Last on a channel, it prints that channel's sound for Combiner, so everyone in the room can drag it into their own session.

Updates

The plug-ins look for a new version when you log in and every 30 minutes. A new one installs by itself, and a notification says so: quit and reopen your DAW to load it. A DAW that's open keeps the version it loaded until then.

The apps check once a day and offer the new version.

To stop the plug-in updates, paste this in Terminal:

bash ~/Library/Application\ Support/Music\ Plugins/update-plugins.sh --uninstall-agent
